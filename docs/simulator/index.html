    About this simulation

    • The heads and rooms are invented. A running head moves its room 1 °F a minute, a room no head moves drifts toward 76 °F, and the fan speed does not change either rate.
    • The stock row applies one rule from Mitsubishi's manual for heads that share an outdoor unit: "the unit selected last goes into standby mode." A person sets each head. It is not stock AUTO. What stock AUTO does, with sources.
    • The MXZ row runs the integration's own planner, timing and fan-hold code from custom_components/mxz_coordinator, the same functions the integration calls in Home Assistant. Its timing follows Home Assistant 2025.11 and later.
    • Neither row measures real equipment, energy, comfort or noise.
    What this page leaves out

    Features with no control here

    • Door and window pauses, Dry, freeze floors and overheat ceilings, hands-off rooms, cloud heads, eco and the season lockouts, a dual setpoint, a room's own drift, and the shared-mode select. MXZ has each of these.

    Timing that differs from Home Assistant

    • Each room moves every 5 simulated seconds, a second after the room before it.
    • These heads show each change at once. In Home Assistant a head can answer late or become unavailable.
    • MXZ sends to one head after another; in Home Assistant the heads' writes can interleave. A command a newer plan replaces before it is sent is not modeled.
    • When MXZ takes a change made at a head while a plan's commands are still going out, Home Assistant can work out the next plan at once. Here the next plan waits for Home Assistant's 10-second cooldown, so a head can get its next command up to 10 seconds later than in Home Assistant.
    • MXZ takes a change made at a head 5 seconds after it. When that starts a plan, Home Assistant finishes taking the change inside the plan that comes next. No MXZ function says so, so this page follows that order by a rule of its own. It shows when two heads are changed at the same moment.
