    About this simulation

    • The heads and rooms are invented. A running head moves its room 1 °F a minute, a room no head moves drifts toward 76 °F, and the fan does not change either rate. Each room moves every 5 simulated seconds, a second after the room before it.
    • The stock row applies only the manual's standby rule quoted above. What stock AUTO does, with sources.
    • The MXZ row runs the integration's own planner, timing and fan-hold code from custom_components/mxz_coordinator, the same functions the integration calls in Home Assistant. Its timing follows Home Assistant 2025.11 and later.
    • Neither row measures real equipment, energy, comfort or noise.
    What this page leaves out
    • Door and window pauses, Dry, freeze floors and overheat ceilings, hands-off rooms, cloud heads, eco and the season lockouts, a dual setpoint, a room's own drift, and the shared-mode select. MXZ has each of these; this page has no control for them.
    • A head that answers late or goes out of sight. These heads show each change at once.
    • MXZ sends to one head after another; in Home Assistant the heads' writes can interleave. A command a newer plan replaces before it is sent is not modeled.
    • When MXZ takes a change made at a head while a plan's commands are still going out, Home Assistant can work out the next plan at once. Here a plan's commands all go out inside it, so the next plan waits for Home Assistant's 10-second cooldown, and a head can get its next command up to 10 seconds later than in Home Assistant.
    • MXZ takes a change made at a head 5 seconds after it and writes it to the room. When that write starts a plan, Home Assistant finishes taking the change inside the plan that comes next. No MXZ function says so, so this page follows that order by a rule of its own. It shows when two heads are changed at the same moment.
